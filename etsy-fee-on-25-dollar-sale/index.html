Etsy keeps $3.21 of the $29.00 the buyer pays — 11.1%. Here is where every cent of it goes, and the postage line that decides whether the order made money.

Why $25 is a specific kind of price

$25 is where the fixed costs stop dominating. The flat $0.45 is 1.8% of the order instead of 3.0% at $15, so the same product earns noticeably more per sale without any price resistance from buyers.

It is also the first price point where a second unit can be offered as an upgrade rather than a discount — two for $45 clears the flat fees and still reads as a bundle.

What to do about it

  • Use $25 as your floor. Below it the flat fees eat the margin faster than volume can replace it.
  • Price postage at cost or above. Charging $4.00 for a $4.60 label costs you $0.60 per order — invisible in Etsy's fee report.
  • Know the break-even. The order stops making money at $21.19 of materials, packaging and your time.
  • Budget for ads deliberately. A $25 order that comes from an offsite ad still keeps 73.9%, so this price can carry a modest ad budget.

Frequently asked

How much does Etsy take on a $25 sale?

On a $25.00 item with $4.00 shipping, a US seller pays $3.21 in total fees: $1.89 transaction fee, $1.12 payment processing and $0.20 listing. That is 11.1% of the $29.00 the buyer pays, leaving a $25.79 deposit.

Is a $25 item worth selling on Etsy?

Yes. At this price the flat fees are 1.8% of the order instead of 3.0%, so $25 is the first price point where a small handmade item has room for materials, packaging and a little profit.

Does charging shipping separately reduce Etsy fees?

No. The 6.5% transaction fee and the 3% processing fee both apply to item price plus shipping, so the total fee is identical whether the money sits in the price or in the shipping line.

What happens if an offsite ad is attributed to the sale?

Etsy adds 15% of the order (12% for shops over $10,000 a year), capped at $100. On this order that is an extra $4.35, which cuts the deposit from $25.79 to $21.44.
