Only if the product still clears its costs after the fee. Offsite ads take 25.8% of an attributed $30.00 order — here is the arithmetic that tells you, per product, whether that is a sale or a subsidy.

The ad fee is charged on the order total including shipping, exactly like the transaction fee. It is capped at $100 per order, so a large order cannot cost more than that in ad fees — which is the one good thing about it.

What offsite ads actually are

Etsy places your listing in ads on Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest and Bing. If someone clicks one of those ads and buys from your shop within 30 days, the order carries the offsite ad fee instead of the normal advertising cost.

The important distinction: this is not a fee you opt into per order. It is attribution. You cannot tell in advance which orders will carry it, so the only safe way to price is to assume the worst case on the products where a 15% hit would hurt.

Is it worth it? The arithmetic that actually decides

Offsite ads are worth it when the ad fee is smaller than the profit you would lose by not having the sale. That sounds obvious, but it means the answer depends entirely on your margin, not on your price:

On the $12.00 order the ad fee is $2.40, which is 17.1% of what you would have received. If materials and postage for that item cost more than $11.63, the ad-driven sale was a loss — you paid Etsy for the privilege of working.

On the $800.00 order the 15% rate would imply $120.00, but the cap stops it at $100.00. Etsy's total cut lands at 22.1% of the order instead of the 24.6% the uncapped rate would produce. High-ticket products are structurally protected here.

The threshold, and why it is not optional

  • Under $10,000 in the last 365 days: 15% per attributed order. You can switch offsite ads off in your shop settings.
  • $10,000 or more in the last 365 days: 12% per attributed order, and it becomes mandatory — you cannot opt out.
  • The $100 cap applies per order either way. It is the only ceiling in the fee stack.

Because the switch from 15% to 12% happens on a rolling 365-day window, a strong Q4 can move you into the mandatory tier for the whole of the following year. That is a permanent change to your unit economics triggered by a good month, and it is worth modelling before you push volume.

How to decide per product

  • Compute the ad-burdened margin at 15%, not at 12%. Assume the worst tier; if the product still works, the 12% tier is a bonus.
  • Kill anything whose materials plus postage exceed the ad-burdened deposit. On a $12.00 order that ceiling is $7.63. Most handmade items at that price do not clear it once postage is paid.
  • Raise the price rather than eat the fee. A 15% ad fee on $30.00 is $5.25; the same fee at $40.00 is $6.90 on a bigger deposit. The percentage is fixed, the flat part is not.
  • Do not confuse it with Etsy Ads. Etsy Ads is a budget you set and pay whether or not it sells. Offsite ads is a cut of sales that happen. The first is a cost you control, the second is margin you discover after the fact.

Frequently asked

What percentage does Etsy take for offsite ads?

15% of the order for shops under $10,000 in the last 365 days, 12% for shops at or above that threshold. It is charged on the order total including shipping and capped at $100 per order.

Can I turn off Etsy offsite ads?

Below $10,000 of annual sales you can switch them off in shop settings. At or above $10,000 they become mandatory and the rate drops to 12%.

Are offsite ads the same as Etsy Ads?

No. Etsy Ads is an advertising budget you set and pay regardless of results. Offsite ads charge a percentage only on orders Etsy attributes to an external ad click, so the cost is discovered after the sale.

Do offsite ad fees apply to shipping?

Yes. Like the transaction and processing fees, the offsite ad fee is calculated on the full order total the buyer pays, including shipping and gift wrap.
