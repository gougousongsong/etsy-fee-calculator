Etsy fee on a $100 sale

Etsy keeps $10.80 of the $109.00 the buyer pays — 9.9%. But the deposit is not your profit, and the shipping line is where this price point quietly loses money.

This is the number most sellers never compute: on a $100 order the deposit is $98.20, but $10.35 of that is postage you owe the carrier. If the buyer paid $9.00 for shipping and the label costs $10.35, the shipping line is losing you $1.35 on every order — before Etsy's cut is even counted a second time.

Why $100 is a specific kind of price

The $0.25 processing component and the $0.20 listing fee are flat. They do not care what you charge. On a $100 order they consume $0.45 of the $100 price, which is 0.5% of the order before a single percentage fee applies. On a $150 order the same flat fees are only 0.3%.

That is the whole reason cheap items lose money on Etsy and the same items bundled make money. Compare the margin at three price points:

What to do about it

  • Price the shipping honestly. If the label costs $10.35, charge at least $10.35. Under-charging for postage is the most common silent loss on Etsy and it does not show up anywhere in Etsy's fee report, because it is not a fee — it is your own margin.
  • Bundle two to clear $150 or more. Two of these at a small discount beats one, because the flat fees are paid once.
  • Know the break-even. On this price the order stops being profitable at $87.85 of materials and packaging. Anything above that is a loss you are choosing.
  • Watch the offsite ads line. An attributed sale at this price drops Etsy's cut from $10.80 to $27.15. That is 16.6% of the money that was going to reach you.

Frequently asked

How much does Etsy take on a $100 sale?

On a $100.00 item with $9.00 shipping, a US seller pays $10.80 in total fees: $7.08 transaction fee, $3.52 payment processing and $0.20 listing. That is 9.9% of the $109.00 the buyer pays, leaving a $98.20 deposit.

Is a $100 item worth selling on Etsy?

Yes, if the materials cost less than $89.20. After fees and a realistic $10.35 shipping label, $87.85 is left to cover materials, packaging and your time. Below roughly $35.00 of materials the margin is thin enough that one return wipes out several sales.

Does charging shipping separately reduce Etsy fees?

No. The 6.5% transaction fee and the 3% processing fee both apply to item price plus shipping, so the total fee is identical whether the money sits in the price or in the shipping line. Only the flat $0.25 and $0.20 are unaffected by how you split it.

What happens to the fee if an offsite ad is attributed to the sale?

Etsy adds 15% of the order (12% for shops over $10,000 a year), capped at $100. On this order that is an extra $16.35, which cuts the deposit from $98.20 to $81.85.
