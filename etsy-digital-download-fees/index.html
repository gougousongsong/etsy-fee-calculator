What is different about selling files

The fee percentages are identical to physical goods: 6.5% transaction plus 3% + $0.25 processing plus $0.20 listing. What changes is that there is no shipping to inflate the fee base and no postage cost to leak margin. In exchange, the flat fees dominate everything below roughly $15.

On a $5.00 file: the transaction fee is $0.33, processing is $0.40, listing is $0.20 — total $0.93, or 18.6% of the sale. Almost half of that is fixed cost that does not shrink if you lower the price.

The maths of bundles

Because digital margin is almost 100% once the file exists, the flat fees are the only real variable. Four separate $5.00 files produce $16.28 of deposits and pay $1.80 in flat fees. One $20.00 bundle of the same four files produces $17.65 and pays $0.45.

Things that quietly cost you money on digital orders

  • Auto-renew on unsold listings. A digital listing that never sells still pays $0.20 every four months. 300 dead listings is $15 a year of pure waste — and it compounds as you add variants.
  • Refunds keep the listing fee. Etsy returns the transaction fee on a cancellation but not the $0.20, so churn costs you money even when nothing ships.
  • Currency conversion, 2.5%. A digital shop priced in one currency selling internationally pays this on converted orders, on top of the standard stack.
  • Offsite ads on files are brutal. A 15% cut on a $10.00 download leaves $7.10, and that order has no postage cost to blame.
  • Support time is a real cost. "It won't open on my phone" emails cost labour that no calculator line captures — price in some of it.

So what should you charge?

Below $10.00, digital pricing on Etsy is mostly a volume game where Etsy's flat fees are a fixed tax on each transaction. Above $20.00 the fee percentage stabilises near 11.3% and the product's perceived value becomes the constraint, not the fee.

The practical rule: never price a standalone file below $5.00 on Etsy unless it is a tripwire for a bundle. And compute the break-even at your real price rather than assuming the 10% everybody quotes — at $3.00 Etsy is taking 24.7%, not 10%.

Frequently asked

Are Etsy fees lower for digital downloads?

The percentages are the same 6.5% transaction and 3% + $0.25 processing. What you save is the shipping line, which lowers the fee base, and the postage cost, which is a real cost rather than a fee. The $0.20 listing fee is unchanged.

How much does Etsy take from a $5 digital download?

Fees total $0.93: $0.33 transaction, $0.40 processing and $0.20 listing. You receive $4.07, which is 18.6% of the sale — most of that being fixed cost.

Is it better to sell one bundle or four separate files?

On identical revenue the bundle nets about $1.37 more, because the $0.25 processing component and the $0.20 listing fee are charged once instead of four times.

Do digital downloads pay the listing fee more than once?

Yes. Every listing pays $0.20 to publish and again on each auto-renewal after four months or after a sale, whether or not the file ever sells.
