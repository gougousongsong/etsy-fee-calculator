The rates used here

  • Listing fee: $0.20 per listing, charged on publishing and again on renewal (a listing lasts 4 months). Extra $0.20 per additional quantity sold in one order.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the order total, including shipping and gift wrap.
  • Payment processing: 3% + $0.25 for US bank accounts; 4% + €0.30 for most of the EU; 4% + £0.20 for the UK. It varies by country.
  • Offsite Ads: 15% of an attributed order if the shop has made under $10,000 in any 365 days, 12% above that, capped at $100 per order.
  • Not included: VAT or regulatory operating fees (UK, France, Italy, India, Spain, Türkiye, Vietnam, Canada), currency conversion at 2.5%, Etsy Ads, and shipping label costs.

Figures taken from Etsy's published fees and payments policies, checked 23 November 2026. Always confirm against your own payment account, because Etsy can change rates and country rules.

Frequently asked

Does Etsy charge the transaction fee on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee applies to the order total, including shipping and gift wrap you charge the buyer.

Why do my fees look higher than 9.5%?

6.5% transaction plus 3% processing is 9.5% before the flat $0.25, the $0.20 listing and any Offsite Ads order fee. Cheap items take a bigger percentage hit because the flat fees do not shrink with price.

Is the Offsite Ads fee charged on every order?

No. It only applies to orders Etsy attributes to an offsite ad click within 30 days, and it is capped at $100 per order.
