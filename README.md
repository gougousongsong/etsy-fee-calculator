## Why these exist

Most fee calculators show one percentage and stop. These include the costs that actually decide whether a product or a job makes money: flat per-order fees that punish cheap items, fee-bearing shipping, storage held over months, advertising charged per unit sold, and a provision for returns.

## Notes on accuracy

Fee rates are checked against published provider policies and dated on each page. Providers change them, and your own account page is always the authority for your region. Each calculator takes the fee as an input where the fee depends on your specific item, so you are never forced to trust an embedded table.
